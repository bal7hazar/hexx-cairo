# [GPT-6-Astra] Audit — LIB-03 — design

## Verdict

FAIL

The plan covers the requested structure and largely preserves the binding decisions, but several algorithms return incorrect results on valid inputs. Gas ceilings, public-API accounting and milestone exit criteria also need correction before L-G2.

| 4 | major | Plan:734, N-2 seam arithmetic | Masking to a row or column does not by itself make subsequent divisions exact, and the contact sets are not disjoint. | The plan asserts `"Every shift is an exact field product"` and `"the union is an addition of disjoint bitmaps"`. | 6 | major | Plan:848–850, N-6 | The canonical-mask placement is incorrect near the upper boundary, and a complete radius-8 canonical shape cannot be represented by the proposed width-15 bitmap. | Plan:850 shifts first and clips high rows afterward.
