## What changed

Milestone L-M2 (#92, #105, #107, #108, #110, #111, #112, #114, #115, #116), on top of `0.1.0-rc.2`, then
the release prep (#117) and the parity fixes after the audit of L-M2 (#118: the texts of the
deviations corrected, `HexSpanExt` re-exported at `hexx::hex` and the crate root). See the
[CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md) section `[0.2.0]`.

- Parity: 344 items ported and 19 renamed, 52.3 % of the 694 items of `hexx` 0.25.0 (10.0 % in
  rc.2); 322 `dropped` with their reasons; 9 `missing`, all of L-M3 (`--check-release L-M2` passes).
- Modules: `hex` (the rest of `HexTrait`, operators, swizzles, euclidean, convert, rings, grid
  edges and vertices), `direction`, `conversions`, `bounds`, `shapes`.
- Deferred, recorded `dropped`: `Sum`/`Product` of `Hex` (M2-T3, #107; `PLAN.md`, *Deferred*):
  corelib's `core::iter::Sum`/`Product` need the experimental feature
  `associated_item_constraints` in the published manifest.
- Results changed: none. Extensions: 240 (unchanged). Deviations: 320 documented (59 in rc.2).
- `DirectionWay::map` takes a closure: a consumer class that calls it has a build-path-dependent
  class hash until cairo#10359 ships in a Scarb (CHANGELOG, *Deviations*).

## What the consumer must do

Nothing: the release is additive and requires the same toolchain as rc.2 (Cairo >= 2.20.0, Scarb
>= 2.20.1). Change the dependency to `hexx = "0.2.0"`. A consumer that calls `DirectionWay::map`
and records class hashes should read the deviation above.
