## Deviations

From the brief, all accepted by the orchestrator's decision 6:

- one keyed table of tuples (`LINES: [(felt252, u8, u8, u8, u8); 374]`) instead of `LINES` plus
  `LINE_SPANS`;
- the loop path walks the board one direction at a time instead of calling `hex_to_index` per
  sample;
- `approach` finds the predecessor by its offset from the target;
- the golden vectors are digests in the field;
- `line_ties.md` is 666 KB.

The resizing of the tests follows decision 4:

- symmetry on 15 × 16 in 8 tests, balanced by number of pairs;
- the ring `approach` on 15 × 16 in 10 tests of 6 ring targets each, the last of 4;
- `line_of_sight` on 7 × 7 split into one test per grid;
- the board golden as described in *Summary*;
- the symmetry and translation unit test of `line_to` on `[-3, 3]²` (2,401 pairs), so that it
  stays under the step limit.
