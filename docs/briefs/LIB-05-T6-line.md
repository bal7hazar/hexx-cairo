## Goal

After this task the board answers the game's line of sight: the tiles strictly between two tiles
of a board, whether walls block the sight between them, and from which side a target is
approached. The line is the integer line of the mirror `Hex`, with the game's tie rule (the lower
tile index), symmetric and translation-invariant; it deviates from `hexx`'s `f32` `line_to` only
where that is documented and listed. It is need N-5 of milestone L-M1, for the game's ENG-02, and
the last extension of release candidate `0.1.0-rc.1`.

## Scope

**In**

1. **The mirror**: `HexTrait::line_to(self: Hex, other: Hex) -> Span<Hex>` in `hex.cairo`, the
   integer rule of §6.6 (no division, no `f32`), with the doc template `Mirrors …` / `#### Panics`
   / `#### Deviations` naming the tie rule and the two `f32` sources of difference.
2. **The board**: `board/line.cairo`, a trait `LineTrait` (D-143) with the plan's three
   signatures and contracts, `self: HexMap` as the plan writes them: `line(self, from: u8, to: u8)
   -> Option<felt252>`, `line_of_sight(self, from, to) -> bool`, `approach(self, from, to) ->
   Option<Direction>`. Two paths, **the table path for `width == 15` and distance ≤ 6 only**, the
   loop path everywhere else (§6.6 "Dispatch"); the result never depends on the path. `approach`
   dispatches `neighbor_mask` (interior target) or `edge_neighbors` (ring target), as §6.6 says.
3. **The tables** `LINES: [felt252; 254]` and `LINE_SPANS: [u8; 254]` (or a layout of your own
   that you prove equal): produced by a committed, re-runnable generator (a `refgen` command or a
   script under `tools/refgen`), never typed by hand; the report gives the command.
4. **Golden vectors and deviations** (`tools/refgen`, §4.3): a `line` spec generated from
   `hexx` 0.25.0: every non-tie pair of the 15 × 16 window in the mirror frame (identity with
   `hexx`), the seeded sample of `[-40, 40]²`, and the adversarial large-coordinate vectors
   (endpoints near `2^k`, `k = 20..=30`, long lines). Every pair where this port and `hexx`
   differ is listed in `docs/deviations/line_ties.md`, generated by `refgen`, under its heading
   (tie, endpoint rounding, interpolation rounding). `cargo run -- check` green.
5. **Tests in the module (D-167)**: the unit tests of `line.cairo` in its `#[cfg(test)] mod
   tests`, those of `line_to` in `hex.cairo`'s; the golden vectors in
   `crates/hexx/tests/golden_line.cairo`. Oracles, all of §6.6's "Oracle" row: the table against
   the loop on every pair within distance 6 of a 15 × 16 board (both parities of start);
   symmetry `line(a, b) == line(b, a)` on every ordered pair of 15 × 16 and of 7 × 7, `None`
   included; `line_of_sight` against a per-tile scalar walk that treats a tile off the board as
   blocked; `approach` against the last ordered sample of the scalar line on every pair of 15 × 16
   with `to` on the ring; R-N5-1 to R-N5-6 with their expected values.
