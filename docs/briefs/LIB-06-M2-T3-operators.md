## Scope

**In**

1. `hex/impls.cairo` — operators as corelib impls: `Add`, `Sub`, `Neg` (`const_add`, `const_sub`,
   `const_neg`), `Mul`, `Div`, `Rem` of `Hex` by `Hex` (per component; `Div` truncates toward zero
   as Rust; a zero component panics), `AddAssign`, `SubAssign`, `MulAssign`, `DivAssign`,
   `RemAssign` (homogeneous), `Sum` and `Product` of an iterator of `Hex` (`ZERO`, `ONE`; the `&Hex`
   variants collapse into the by-value ones, §4.4) — through the corelib traits of the toolchain;
   if it has none for `Sum`/`Product`, escalate, do not invent a trait. Named counterparts on a
   trait `HexOpsTrait` (D-143): `add_scalar`, `sub_scalar` (`Add<i32>`, `Sub<i32>`),
   `add_direction`, `sub_direction` (`EdgeDirection`), `add_diagonal`, `sub_diagonal`
   (`VertexDirection`: `self.const_add(rhs.into_hex())`, not through `add_diag_dir` of M2-T2),
   `div_scalar`, `rem_scalar`. `mul_scalar` is M2-T0's. Absent: the bitwise and shift operators,
   the heterogeneous assignment operators, every `f32` operand (§4.4).
2. **`div_scalar(self, rhs: i32) -> Hex`, the contract** (normative; the method is yours):
   - `hexx` computes `n = length / rhs` (`i32`, truncated toward zero), then
     `ZERO.lerp(self, n as f32 / length as f32)` rounded by `Hex::round`. The counterpart computes
     the same rescale **on exact rationals**: the target `(x·n/L, y·n/L)` with `L = self.length()`,
     rounded by `Hex::round`'s rule (each component rounded half away from zero; the larger
     remainder, compared with `>=`, takes `round(r_a + r_b / 2)` — `:474-484`), all exact.
   - Domain and panics: `rhs = 0` panics (as `hexx`'s integer division); `L = 0` returns `ZERO`
     (`hexx`: `0/0` is NaN and NaN casts to 0 — confirm on the vectors); an `i32` overflow panics
     (§3.1).
   - Oracle: **off-chain, exhaustive** in `refgen`: every `Hex` of `[-40, 40]²` against every
     `rhs` of `[-12, 12] \ {0}` (157,464 pairs), plus a seeded sample of 4,096 pairs with
     `2^20 ≤ L < 2^31`; `refgen` writes every pair where `hexx` and the exact rule differ to
     `docs/deviations/div_scalar.md` (committed, verified by `-- check`, as `line_ties.md`), each
     line with `hexx`'s result, the rule's result and why (`f32` error). On-chain golden tests: a
     seeded sample of 512 non-deviating pairs, every pair with `|x|, |y| ≤ 6` and `rhs ∈ [-6, 6]`,
     and the regression cases below.
   - Regression cases: `ZERO.div_scalar(k) = ZERO` for `k ∈ {1, -1, 7}`; `rhs > L` gives `ZERO`;
     `rhs = -1` gives `-self`; `rhs = 1` gives `self`; `hex(3, 0).div_scalar(2)`,
     `hex(1, 1).div_scalar(2)` and one exact hexround tie per sign, with the values the crate gives.
   - `rem_scalar(self, rhs) = self − div_scalar(self, rhs).mul_scalar(rhs)` (`:298`), same domain,
     inheriting the deviation; `Rem<Hex>` is per component and has none.
   - If the exact rule and `hexx` differ on a pair that is **not** explained by `f32` error, or the
     deviation list exceeds 1 % of the exhaustive domain, stop and report: the plan's restatement
     would be wrong.
3. `hex/swizzle.cairo`, `HexSwizzleTrait`: `xx`, `yy`, `zz`, `yx`, `yz`, `xz`, `zx`, `zy`.
